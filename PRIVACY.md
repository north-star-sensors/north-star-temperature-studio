# Privacy Policy

**North Star Temperature Studio**

_Last updated: June 2, 2026_

North Star Temperature Studio ("the app") is developed and published by
North Star Sensors ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what the app does
and does not do with your information.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.**
Everything the app records stays on your device, under your control. We operate
no servers and never receive your data.

## Information the app handles

The app works entirely on your device. It may store the following **locally on
your device only**:

- **Temperature sensor readings** captured from a connected sensor or the
  built-in simulator, including any recording sessions and markers you create.
- **App settings and preferences** (such as calibration values and display
  options).
- **Files you export** (CSV or Excel), saved to the location you choose.

This information is never sent to us or to any third party. It remains on your
device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to any of the following:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, account, or device ID)
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
- Usage analytics or advertising identifiers
- Crash or diagnostic reports

The app contains **no advertising**, **no third-party analytics**, and
**no user accounts or sign-in**.

## Device access the app uses

- **USB / serial access** — used solely to communicate with North Star
  temperature sensor hardware. No data from this connection leaves your device.

The app does not request access to your location, microphone, camera, or
contacts, and it does not send your data over the internet.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we never receive it in
the first place. Any sharing of files you export (for example, emailing a CSV
you created) is initiated by you, using apps and services you choose, and is
outside the scope of this app.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for users **aged 13 and older** and is not directed to
children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone,
it collects none from minors.

## Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete recording sessions
within the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data.
We retain nothing, because we never receive your data.

## Open source

North Star Temperature Studio is open-source software. You can review exactly
how it handles data by reading the source code:

https://github.com/north-star-sensors/north-star-temperature-studio

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post
the revised policy at this same location.
